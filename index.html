About Us

Crafted With Heritage. Designed For The Future.

Maison Nirya began as a single embroidery table in an old Lucknow workshop. Two decades on, the same hand-worked techniques sit alongside a modern eye for silhouette — every piece still passes through the hands of artisans who learned their craft from the generation before them.

Heritage

Centuries of zardozi and gota patti preservation.

Devotion

400–600 hours of continuous hand needlework.

Modern Fit

Boned foundations built for effortless movement.
